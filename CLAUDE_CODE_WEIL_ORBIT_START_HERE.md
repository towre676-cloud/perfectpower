The supplied orbit package has been integrated and extended. `docs/WEIL_LOCAL_DIMENSION_MONOGRAPH.md` gives paper proofs of the all-exponent dimension formulas, transpose symmetry of every commutant matrix, commutativity, and multiplicity-free complex decomposition. The group premises have primary references and the mixed theta-image independence step is spelled out.

`python/perfectpower/weil_orbit.py` traverses phase-labelled orbits, excludes inconsistent orbits from the sparse basis, checks support and transpose phases, and independently checks small-level products by exact cyclotomic twisted convolution. Invalid levels and quadratic resource bounds are checked before allocation. `weil_orbit_certificate` is available through `query_service.dispatch`, with `args={"level":128}` giving dimension 13 and execution_verified=false.

Run `make check-weil-orbit`. It regenerates 166 finite exact phase/reflection receipts, reconciles all 63 earlier certified dimensions, checks eight exact product levels, runs ten tests and audits twelve general Lean declarations. These declarations prove six gauge identities, finite orbit-sum symmetry, span transport, commutant closure and the implication from symmetric spanning vectors to commutativity. They do not yet formalize the concrete all-level orbit classification.
