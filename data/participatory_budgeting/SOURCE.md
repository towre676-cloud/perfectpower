# Public election snapshot

These are four complete UTF-8 Pabulib `.pb` files from [pabulib/pabulib_files](https://github.com/pabulib/pabulib_files/tree/4f8497afb2168e732d42f4960307e4a85f9dcc7b/pb_files), pinned at commit `4f8497afb2168e732d42f4960307e4a85f9dcc7b`. See [Pabulib format](https://pabulib.org/format) and [project attribution](https://pabulib.org/about). Original file bytes, including line endings, are preserved. `manifest.json` records source URLs, Git blob identities and SHA-256 hashes. Public source data retain their original attribution; the repository's code license does not assign a new license to third-party data.

Selection used supported vote types and project counts, before measuring performance: Toulouse 2019 (30 projects, cumulative), Amsterdam Bloemenbuurt 2020 (24, approval), Amsterdam Rijnbuurt West 2022 (15, approval), and Warszawa Wesoła 2023 (29, approval). They are four cases from a much larger collection, not a representative sample of all elections. No projects or ballots were removed and no costs or support totals were synthesized.

Preserve source caveats. Rijnbuurt's source assigns cost 1 to two otherwise free projects; Budget Lab retains that published cost. Amsterdam metadata describe weighting; published project totals remain authoritative, and ballot aggregation discrepancies are surfaced rather than silently replacing them. All four selected files currently have matching published and aggregated totals. Wesoła's 2023 edition records voting in June 2022. Category spending limits in Bloemenbuurt are enforced in addition to total budget.

Budget Lab computes analytical portfolios maximizing total published support. It does not reproduce the city's official greedy rule, establish a fairness criterion, or recommend a municipal decision. A voter supporting several selected projects contributes several times to this additive objective; distinct represented ballots are reported separately. Original official selection flags are displayed only when provided by the source.
