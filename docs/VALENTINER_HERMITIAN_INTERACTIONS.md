# Hermitian flavor sources and exact one-loop mass-phase cancellation

## The unified canonical result

A real Hermitian scalar nonet supplies a useful interaction constraint: every scalar variation of the heavy-to-light mixing block remains Hermitian. With a scalar universal heavy mass and Higgs Yukawa, the complete renormalizable fermion action then has a positive tree determinant and an exactly vanishing one-loop determinant phase. The cancellation holds for arbitrary real neutral-scalar mixing, including Higgs/flavor portals. The same canonical action realizes six hierarchical light masses and four finite-scale charged-current targets. No field-dependent quark metric is used in that benchmark.

The mass and current construction uses explicit fitted real source-center coefficients. It establishes compatibility of hierarchy, weak CP and one-loop mass-phase cancellation in one action; it does not derive the golden CKM relation. The scientific receipt retains those coefficients, all 128 branches of a separate mass-only affine search, and twelve fixed-mass deformations. The latter identify exactly where predictive freedom survives the one-loop theorem.

## A low-degree invariant basis with an exact determinant

Let R = A A-dagger/r_scale and T = L L-dagger/t_scale in the up-family triplet space, with fixed positive normalization constants. Both transform by conjugation. The following nine Hermitian matrix words have real coefficients and obey the elementary generalized CP transformation without an inserted imaginary unit: I, R, R-squared, T, RT+TR, R-squared T+T R-squared, T-squared, RT-squared+T-squared R, and RT-squared R. Their elementary scalar degrees are respectively 0,2,4,2,4,6,4,6,8.

Use coordinates consisting of three real diagonal entries and the real and imaginary parts of entries 12,13,23. At a nondegenerate R eigenbasis define Delta = (r2-r1)(r3-r1)(r3-r2) and tau = Im(T12 T23 T31). The determinant of the nine-word coordinate matrix is Delta-cubed times tau-cubed. Equivalently, if chi = Im Tr(R-squared T-squared RT), then chi = Delta tau and the coordinate determinant is chi-cubed. With orthonormal Hilbert–Schmidt off-diagonal coordinates the determinant is 8 chi-cubed. Nonzero chi therefore provides a complete real Hermitian source-center basis through scalar degree eight.

The symbolic certificate factorizes the diagonal Vandermonde determinant Delta, two off-diagonal coefficient determinants -Delta and +Delta, and three real pair determinants -tau,+tau,-tau. ## An exact one-loop phase theorem

In the source eigenbasis the left and right fermion rotations are real two-state matrices. Every physical neutral real-scalar mode has a Hermitian source variation A, plus a real flavor-diagonal Higgs vertex. A mass-basis source vertex connecting states (i,sigma) and (j,tau) therefore equals a real two-state factor times A_ij. For i different from j, its chirality-flipping paired product G_IK G_KI is a real factor times |A_ij|-squared. Within a single flavor, A_ii and every Higgs contribution are real. Every paired product is thus real separately, including interference between the Higgs and nonet portions of an arbitrarily mixed mode.

The one-loop diagonal chirality-changing self-energy is a sum of these paired products times real masses and real loop weights. Its imaginary part vanishes term by term. Hence Im Tr(D-inverse delta D) = 0 exactly for every real neutral-scalar mixing matrix and positive scalar spectrum, without a small spurion or a cancellation between sectors. The same argument cancels the UV phase coefficient. Generic tests with 91 independently mixed modes give only floating-point errors of order 10^-15; these errors are checks of the analytic identity, not a predicted residual theta.

Neutral gauge phase factors use Hermitian left currents and real diagonal right currents. The full charged and neutral Goldstone Ward products reduce to real squared-current magnitudes. Their reality is checked with all six states per sector. Charged gauge loops contribute Hermitian wavefunction factors. Scalar tadpole shifts preserve Hermitian C and the identically real tree determinant. These observations close the first-order one-loop mass-phase cancellation for the specified complete renormalizable fermion action and canonical bare metrics.

This theorem concerns the first-order one-loop determinant phase. Higher fermion or field-dependent kinetic vertices and two-loop corrections are outside its assumptions. It is not an all-loop strong-CP solution. One-loop-vanishing Nelson–Barr extensions already exist in the literature; see Alves and Nishi, arXiv:2506.03257 / JHEP 09 (2025) 162. No priority claim is made for that general possibility. The concrete result here is the real-nonet interaction, termwise proof with arbitrary scalar mixing, and exact finite hierarchy/current construction in the same canonical action.
