# Research receipt policy

A `THEOREM_EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY` receipt must name the exact imported theorem and the step to which it applies. The polynomial 0–1 proof depends on Boshernitzan's uniform-distribution criterion in its nonrigid branch; the shifted nonsingular-cubic finiteness statement depends on Siegel's theorem. A `PAPER_PROOF` records a written mathematical argument. `EXACT_COMPUTATION` records parameters, range, exit code, and reproducible integer calculations. `FINITE_HIT_CERTIFICATE` records rational polynomial identities and inequalities proving the absence of hits beyond a cutoff. `LEAN_VERIFIED` is reserved for compiled checked declarations against a named immutable dependency revision. This release has no `LEAN_VERIFIED` receipt.

`exact_benchmarks.json` is an `EXACT_COMPUTATION` result. Its counts are exact over the recorded ranges; no numerical `limsup` is asserted. A certificate's `exact_identity` means polynomial equality was checked, whereas a nonidentity valid cutoff means no hit at or above its cutoff by the explicitly checked inequalities. A nonrigid classification is neither a certificate of infinite hits nor a rate estimate. For finite surgery, the code constructs the modified sequence by finitely many overrides; the exact integer D then proves the count identity for all subsequent N. A finite-range check of two arbitrary source functions alone would not.

The former receipts are recast as follows. Periodic zeta residue is a theorem under periodicity. Periodic heat density is a special case of an Abelian theorem for every existing ordinary density. Tropical finite-window maximum is an elementary observation. Periodic entropy zero is elementary. A cofinite-tail Čech construction that only restates invariance of limsup under finite-prefix change is tautological. The BSD link for a shifted nonsingular cubic is an unconditional Siegel application and does not use L(E,1). These are mathematical status labels, not claims that this repo has compiled their Lean formalizations.
