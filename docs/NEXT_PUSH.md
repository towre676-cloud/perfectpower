# Next research moves after the integrated push

The first objective is to compile the small Lean kernel against an immutable Mathlib revision. Repair `Basic` and `Density` until the definitions, convergence bridge, bounded finite support, exact surgery, and periodic rational density have build logs. Then attack the rigid branch in independent pieces: rational truncated roots, integral closure, denominator discreteness, a degree-to-evaluation bound, and the nonzero-remainder cutoff. The Python certificate can serve as a reference for a future proof-by-reflection tactic, but it is not itself a Lean theorem. Formalize the nonrigid density branch only after locating or proving a sufficient equidistribution criterion inside the formal library; until then retain the external-paper boundary.

The arithmetic research priority within H=0 is an atlas indexed by polynomial degree, target exponent, factorization/genus of y^d=F(x), local congruence solvability, and an effective or non-effective hit-count bound. Exact formula families should be used first: monomials, linear congruences, Pell-type quadratics, and rigid finite-cutoff families. For nonsingular cubics, a finiteness theorem is stronger than density zero, but a complete enumeration needs additional effective methods. For nonrigid F with an irrational-leading root or fractional-power root, seek explicit discrepancy estimates with constants and hypotheses, then translate discrepancy into counts of exact integer values without assuming a fixed-width interval yields a power saving automatically.

The analytic priority is to prove the count-to-transform statements from summation by parts and then distinguish Abelian directions from Tauberian converses. Determine when A(N)∼cN^α(log N)^β yields a leading heat asymptotic and a Dirichlet singularity, and treat α=0 finite and Pell-like infinite support separately. Avoid calling a real-axis Abelian limit a meromorphic residue. The numerical priority is to generate exact, hashable receipts on increasing dyadic ranges and instrument runtime/memory without ever allowing a finite maximum to masquerade as H. The natural-sequence frontier, including factorial expressions, needs separately justified theorems; the polynomial dichotomy does not transfer to those sequences by analogy.
