# All-level Fourier/chirp dimensions and a commutative orbit algebra

The subsequent [concrete Heisenberg bridge](WEIL_MONOMIAL_MONOGRAPH.md) now proves the literal basis, corrected generator actions, concrete coefficient membership equivalence and odd-level orbit-quotient dimension law in Lean. References below to those steps as future work describe this paper release; the arithmetic orbit classification and even support reduction remain to be formalized.

## The result and its proof status

Use the repository convention F[x,y] = zeta_N raised to xy and T[x,x] = zeta_N raised to x squared. F is unnormalized. The chirp uses an Nth root at even levels as well as odd levels. Write c(N) for the dimension of their simultaneous commutant over Q(zeta_N). Level one is included, with c(1)=1.

For odd N the dimension is the divisor count tau(N). If N=2^a m with m odd and a positive, the dimension is (2a-1) tau(m). Moreover, every matrix in this commutant is symmetric under ordinary transposition. The commutant is therefore commutative. Over the complex numbers, the unitary representation generated by normalized F and T is multiplicity-free, with exactly c(N) pairwise non-isomorphic irreducible constituents.

$$
c(N)=\tau(N)\quad(N\ \mathrm{odd}),\qquad c(2^a m)=(2a-1)\tau(m).
$$

These are paper theorems under the classical group facts identified below. The supplied orbit draft establishes the dimension argument; this extension supplies the transpose-reflection proof of commutativity and its multiplicity-free consequence. No novelty claim is made for the underlying classical representation theory. Twelve general Lean declarations prove the gauge identities and algebraic symmetry bridge. They do not yet prove the all-level orbit classification or its instantiation on the Fourier/chirp commutant.

The exact finite verifier checks all levels 1 through 160 and the additional levels 243, 256, 343, 512, 625 and 729. All 166 levels agree with the formula and satisfy the phase-sensitive transpose criterion. It agrees with the 63 pre-existing cyclotomic basis/rank-minor certificates. A separate exact twisted-convolution calculation checks eight smaller levels. These finite calculations are evidence and regression targets, not substitutes for the general proof. Python results retain execution_verified=false.

## The monomial action and its fixed space

Set X_s e_x=e_(x+s), Z_t e_x=zeta raised to tx times e_x, and E_(s,t)=Z_t X_s. The E matrices form a basis of the full matrix algebra: for each shift, the N clock characters form a Fourier basis of its supported entries. Character orthogonality proves their independence over the cyclotomic field.

Direct calculation gives the two conjugation identities below. The sign in the chirp phase is negative; reversing the order Z_t X_s would change the convention.

$$
F E_{s,t}F^{-1}=\zeta^{st}E_{-t,s},\qquad
T E_{s,t}T^{-1}=\zeta^{-s^2}E_{s,t+2s}.
$$

Thus conjugation acts by permutations of the Heisenberg basis with root-of-unity multipliers. An orbit contributes one fixed vector if its accumulated phases are consistent, and zero fixed vectors otherwise. Indeed, one coefficient determines every coefficient along a path; a loop with nontrivial accumulated phase forces that coefficient to vanish. A consistent assignment yields an invariant orbit sum, unique up to scalar. Different orbits have disjoint Heisenberg support. Their surviving sums form a basis of the simultaneous commutant.

The Python traversal assigns phases along a spanning tree and checks every outgoing generator edge. An inconsistent orbit is retained in the census but excluded from the basis. The traversal bounds N squared before allocation and never uses floating-point tolerances.

## Odd levels and the first dimension formula

For odd N choose h with 2h=1 modulo N and gauge the Heisenberg basis by the phase -hst. Both conjugations then permute the gauged basis without phases, because the change in the gauge is precisely st under rotation and -s squared under the chirp shear.

$$
W_{s,t}=\zeta^{-hst}E_{s,t}.
$$

Every orbit survives. The chirp shear is (s,t) mapped to (s,t+2s). Its hth power is the elementary shear (s,t) mapped to (s,t+s). Together with rotation (s,t) mapped to (-t,s), this generates SL_2(Z/N). Generation follows by reducing the classical integer generators and using surjectivity of reduction.

The invariant content is gcd(s,t,N). For content d, divide the vector by d and work modulo N/d. A unimodular vector can be completed to a determinant-one matrix, so SL_2 is transitive on such vectors. Surjectivity from modulus N to N/d lifts the required transformation. There is one orbit for each divisor d of N, including d=N for zero. This proves c(N)=tau(N).

## Even levels and the surviving sublattice

For even N the power T raised to N/2 is the sign clock Z_(N/2). Its Fourier conjugate is the half-period shift X_(N/2). A matrix commuting with F and T must commute with both. Conjugation of E_(s,t) by these two operators multiplies it by (-1)^s and (-1)^t respectively. In characteristic zero, its coefficient must vanish if either index is odd.

The even-index sublattice is preserved by rotation and the chirp shear. Write N=2M, s=2u and t=2v, with u,v modulo M. The gauge exponent -st/2 becomes -2uv modulo N. It is well defined: changing u by M changes the exponent by -Nv, and similarly for v. The two phase identities hold exactly. Hence every orbit in this sublattice survives and every orbit outside it dies.

$$
W_{2u,2v}=\zeta^{-2uv}E_{2u,2v}.
$$

The surviving index action is rotation together with the even shear on (Z/M)^2. It is the image of the theta group H generated by S and the squared elementary shear. Consequently c(N) is the number of H-orbits on this smaller square, not on the original full square.

## Theta orbits and the dyadic formula

For M=2^b, zero is one orbit. A nonzero vector has content 2^j, with j<b. After dividing by that content, it is primitive modulo 2^(b-j). Its parity type is either exactly one odd coordinate or two odd coordinates. Rotation interchanges the two one-odd patterns; the even shear preserves them up to that interchange.

These two classes are each transitive. To compare primitive vectors with the same parity, complete each to a determinant-one matrix. Adjust the second columns by multiples of the first to make the completions have the same reduction modulo two. Their quotient is then congruent to the identity modulo two. Lift it to an integer determinant-one matrix using reduction surjectivity. Its parity is still the identity, so it lies in Gamma(2), which is contained in H. Rotation handles the swapped one-odd patterns. Reduction surjectivity also lifts transformations through the larger modulus when the content was divided out.

There are two orbits for each valuation j from zero through b-1, plus zero. Thus the orbit count is 2b+1, including one when b=0. With M=N/2, this proves c(2^a)=2a-1.

For M=2^b m with m odd and b positive, the theta image splits into the product of its dyadic image and the full SL_2(Z/m). Here is the independence argument. Any element of SL_2(Z/m) can be combined with the identity modulo 2^b by ring CRT; lift the resulting determinant-one matrix modulo 2^b m to SL_2(Z). The lift is congruent to the identity modulo 2^b, hence lies in Gamma(2) and in H. This supplies the entire odd factor independently. Combining it with lifts of dyadic theta elements supplies the full product of images. Orbit counts therefore multiply. When b=0, the even shear is invertible to the elementary shear modulo odd m, so the image is simply the full group.

The classical premises are generation of SL_2(Z), surjectivity of its reduction maps, and H being the inverse image of the identity-or-swap subgroup modulo two. Keith Conrad's notes [SL2(Z)](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/grouptheory/SL%282%2CZ%29.pdf), Theorems 1.1 and 3.2 and Example 3.7, prove these facts. Noam Elkies's [Math 272 notes of September 23, 2019](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~elkies/M272.19/sep23.pdf), page 7, give the equivalent theta-group characterization. Unimodular completion over the residue ring is elementary Bezout arithmetic. These facts supply the stated premises; the orbit and commutant conclusions are derived here.

## Reflection makes every orbit sum symmetric

Ordinary transposition of the ungauged Heisenberg matrix is

$$
E_{s,t}^{\mathsf{T}}=\zeta^{st}E_{-s,t}.
$$

This follows immediately from its single supported entry in each row. For odd N, the gauged coefficient after transpose is -hst+st=hst, precisely the coefficient of W_(-s,t). For even indices it is -st/2+st=st/2, again the reflected gauge. Thus transposition reflects the gauged index without a residual phase.

$$
W_{s,t}^{\mathsf{T}}=W_{-s,t}.
$$

The reflection (s,t) mapped to (-s,t) preserves every orbit. At odd levels, it preserves content and content completely classifies the orbits. On the surviving even sublattice, it preserves the content of the odd factor and both the dyadic valuation and parity type. Those are precisely the theta-orbit invariants established above. This includes M=1 and zero without exceptions.

Each commutant basis element is the sum of W over one surviving orbit. Reflection merely permutes its terms, so every such sum is symmetric. By linearity, every commutant matrix is symmetric. This is an ordinary transpose statement, not a conjugate-transpose or positivity claim.

## From symmetry to commutativity and multiplicity-free decomposition

The simultaneous commutant is an algebra. If C and D belong to it, then CD does too. All three matrices are symmetric, while transposition reverses multiplication. Therefore

$$
CD=(CD)^{\mathsf{T}}=D^{\mathsf{T}}C^{\mathsf{T}}=DC.
$$

This proves commutativity for every positive level. It replaces the floating-point observation in the supplied draft with a general paper proof. The Lean theorem `commutative_of_orbit_span` proves this implication with the orbit spanning and transpose hypotheses explicitly exposed. `orbit_sum_symmetric` proves the permutation-of-terms step, and the six gauge theorems prove the odd and even phase identities. The finite verifier checks the actual phases, not only orbit membership.

Over the complex numbers, F divided by the square root of N and T are unitary. Every invariant subspace has an invariant orthogonal complement, so the representation is a direct sum of irreducibles. By Schur's lemma its commutant is the direct sum of matrix algebras whose sizes are the irreducible multiplicities. Commutativity forces every size to be one. The number of irreducible constituents is consequently c(N). Extending the cyclotomic coefficient field to the complex numbers preserves the rank of the finite commutator equations and thus their kernel dimension.

This conclusion counts constituents; it does not construct their explicit primitive idempotents, characters or dimensions. Such a spectral decomposition is a further computational and formal target. It is not necessary for either the dimension formula or commutativity.
