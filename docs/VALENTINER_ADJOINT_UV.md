The quark-source contractions have one universal column coupling per sector. Every allowed renormalizable scalar channel on the declared zero-matter/Higgs slice is present with a nonzero coefficient. The angular field, golden coefficient, CKM mixing anchors and observed quark masses are not input targets of this construction. The physical outputs come from minimizing the potential and then canonically matching the heavy quarks.

There are 35 complex flavor scalars, hence 70 real directions. At the retained solution the largest scaled stationarity residual is below 10 to the power -10. These are numerical stationary-point and stability results, supported by independent derivative and symmetry checks, rather than an interval-arithmetic proof of an exact root.

This benchmark is an existence construction, not a fit of the CKM matrix.
