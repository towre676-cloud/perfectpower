The nonorthogonal route is dynamically possible in the declared field content. This calculation enumerates the entire independent CP-even scalar polynomial space through degree six, activates every coefficient, and solves the full 54-real-field stationarity equations. It obtains positive local spectra, small nonzero mixing and physical weak CP. A shared-heavy-ray example also gives the ordering |Vub|<|Vcb|<|Vus|. Neither example reproduces the measured CKM matrix, derives the quark-column hierarchy, selects a global vacuum or predicts a golden coefficient.

The decisive constraint comes from the allowed parameter space. Fully re-minimized one-percent scalar deformations directly change the effective coefficient, also after mass matching. The remaining task is to derive correlations among the interactions and quark weights, rather than extend an unconstrained potential search.

## Why nonorthogonality changes the protection problem

There is an exact elementary theorem behind the numerical sensitivity. For a fixed full-rank column matrix C, let H(t)=sum_i t_i c_i c_i-dagger, with all t_i independently positive. If its unordered nondegenerate left eigenprojectors stay fixed on an open parameter set, then differentiating H with respect to each t_i shows that every rank-one matrix c_i c_i-dagger is diagonal in that same eigenbasis. They therefore commute. Two nonzero rank-one projectors commute only when their vectors are parallel or orthogonal; full rank excludes parallel columns. The columns must be pairwise orthogonal. Conversely orthogonal columns share their projectors throughout every fixed mass-order region.

Thus nonorthogonal sources solve one problem and introduce another obligation: the quark-column coefficients affect the mixing frame. The scalar potential on the zero-matter/Higgs slice does not determine those coefficients. This theorem applies directly to the universal up mediator and to both sectors on a scalar aligned link. On the deformed link the fully matched numerical calculation establishes the response. The theorem concerns the full frame; it does not by itself rule out a specially protected single observable identity at another exceptional configuration.

## The next interaction must constrain both sectors

An unconstrained joint-potential search can now be distinguished from a flavor prediction. The declared field content admits nonorthogonal stable vacua, weak CP and hierarchy mechanisms. Its complete low-degree operator space and independent quark weights leave all four physical mixing parameters adjustable on the tested branch. Finding a golden-looking point in that space would select couplings rather than explain a relation.

The next construction needs a justified non-Abelian action on source labels or a UV interaction whose unique invariant contractions correlate the quark-column weights and the nine mixed scalar coefficients. It must retain its allowed norm-square operators and kinetic corrections in the audit. A larger Abelian phase group cannot forbid the X_ij. New fields are useful only if their full operator space reduces the demonstrated physical freedom. No specific new representation is asserted here to achieve that reduction.
