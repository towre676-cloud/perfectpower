# Lean formalization of the parallel geometry and deep-gems pushes

This push reads the parallel work through commit `62caf6a50382430f3ff1420317608675645b15ae` (including the geometry/deep-gems work at `9bf8506`) and replaces several explicit “no new Lean theorem” gaps with kernel-checked algebra, enumeration theorems and concrete exact certificate checks. Lean is 4.20.0 with the repository's pinned Mathlib. No Sage, Singular or numerical matrix inverse enters the proofs.

`PowerComposition.complete` proves complete original integer point enumeration under a polynomial power-coordinate substitution, for arbitrary nonzero coordinate exponent and arbitrary value exponent. It uses the already proved signed binary-root algorithm, so negative odd fibres, the zero fibre and non-power outer coordinates are handled correctly. `QuarticPowerAtlas` specializes this to both existing quartic solver families. The generated registry records exactly the 3,080 parameter rows of `receipts/divisor_sum/CompleteQuartics.lean`; Lean checks every validity predicate and proves the finite-algorithm equivalence for every row and every nonzero coordinate exponent, including all 6,160 degree-eight/twelve equations in the parallel atlas.

Each of the 6,160 new literal packets also has its own `decide +kernel` lifting equality in the generated `QuarticLiftPackets*` modules. These checks use the saved literal outer packets, whose completeness and packet equalities were proved in the earlier quartic-ledger push. They do not re-run its expensive interval enumerations. The new registry theorem independently proves completeness of the finite solver output; the literal packet checks verify the power-root transformation of the earlier literal outputs. The new modules deliberately do not assert a fresh imported theorem equating each historic literal outer packet to its full interval solver. The prior proof ledger remains the evidence for that historic equality. This distinction is recorded rather than hiding an external certificate assumption.

The enhanced-machinery push arrived during this formalization and is preserved in the parent history. `AffinePowerComposition.complete` proves complete lifting under (sy)^d=H((ax+b)^q), with nonzero a,s,q and both necessary divisibility restrictions a|(t−b), s|v. The proof cancels the nonzero scale power and recovers the original integer pair, including negative coordinates. `AffineQuarticAtlas.complete` supplies the inherited complete quartic leaf theorem for every registered row, every nonzero affine slope and every nonzero coordinate exponent. A further 6,160 literal affine packet equalities are independently kernel-checked from the newly pushed corpus, and the 6,160 plain corpus rows are checked against the already covered plain packets. This covers the transport outputs of all 12,320 stored equations. It does not verify the Python normal-form recognizer, cache implementation, expanded-coefficient proposal generator or its separate rational-truncation height checker. The expanded input still needs its polynomial identity checked before applying the formal transport theorem.

`ResidueCover` proves preservation of solutions by any compatible ring-valued local table and the global empty-cover obstruction. The enhanced example y²=2x⁴+3 receives a complete kernel-checked table modulo 16 and an unconditional theorem of no integer solutions, without a height bound. General table construction, joining several covers and filtered interval-scan replay remain separate implementation-proof targets.

`IntegerOptimization` proves symmetric quadratic cross terms, exact completion of the square and the continuous lower bound under a stated nonnegative Gram-energy hypothesis. The tied example x+y=1 has a proved minimum energy of 1 and exactly the two recorded integer minimizers (0,1),(1,0). Saturated Smith-fibre construction, the LLL transcript verifier, LDL construction, complete ellipsoid enumeration, node budgets and all stored seven-channel optimizer results have not been formally verified. The module proves the algebraic foundation and the tied example; it does not relabel the exact Python optimization engine as Lean-verified.

`PSGRecovery` encodes both recovered expressions independently and proves the actual identity Q(L²W,L,Z)=L¹²R(W,L,Z). It proves the zero-scale identity, the nonzero-scale root equivalence, the specialized sextic coefficients and the failed reversal coefficient test. This is a polynomial identity proof, not a claim that every specialization has been solved.

`WeightedHodge` proves the weighted adjoint equation, a supplied inverse-Gram projector's column-fixing, idempotence and weighted self-adjointness, and the complementary harmonic projector identities for mutually annihilating component projectors. It also exhibits the regularized non-projector counterexample. The six saved rational models, in genera 0,1,2,3,4,7, receive exact checks of both component idempotences, both cross-products, the identity decomposition, harmonic annihilation by the recorded Laplacian, mass self-adjointness and harmonic trace 2g. Harmonic idempotence is then deduced from the general theorem. Larger matrices are checked in row blocks to keep the kernel reductions within memory. A trace value here is a proved matrix trace; no general rank-equals-trace theorem or arbitrary-complex Hodge decomposition is silently assumed.

`DivisorCoordinates` proves the complete integer image criterion and entire solution fibre for D diag(g) Dᵀ with supplied two-sided integral inverse E of D. The criterion is coordinatewise divisibility of Eb by g, and includes zero coefficients correctly. It proves the diagonal Gram energy, nonnegative energy for nonnegative raw weights, and the shared-divisor expansion of a GCD kernel. The normalized positive-weight counterexample evaluates to −4/5, so raw positivity cannot be transferred to that normalization without an additional argument.

`SymplecticTransport` proves recovery and inverse transport of a supplied alternating form and exact bilinear transport under a supplied basis change, over any commutative ring. The seven stored basis records receive separate integer kernel checks of both inverse identities, symplectic congruence and skew symmetry. One record repeats a CLI fixture. These are exact matrix theorems; they do not prove that a numerical mesh cycle is the corresponding smooth algebraic curve homology class.

`PolyhedralVoronoi` proves the exact triangle-gradient Cauchy bound from the positive Gram determinant, an upper-square-root bound, and the strict patch winner/tie exclusion rule in any pseudometric space. The patch rule requires the stated distance enclosure and radius hypotheses. Global continuity, gluing, the Lipschitz property of a proposed mesh function, complete Voronoi partition coverage, and comparison with a smooth canonical metric still need their own proofs.

`FiniteWeilAlgebra` proves diagonal-chirp support restrictions, the full commutant of an injective diagonal clock, shift invariance and scalarity for a transitive clock/shift pair. It proves the integer CRT phase/chirp congruences with the twists retained. It does not certify the parallel engine's separate cyclotomic Fourier/chirp commutant dimensions at every stored level, or every degeneracy map. A chirp with repeated eigenvalues does not satisfy the injective-clock hypothesis automatically.

`PowerSumRecovery` verifies both recovered primitive identities, including the quartic identity with target 422481 and the quintic identity with target 144. It proves exact meet-in-the-middle decompositions and the strict summand bound. It does not promote the bounded experimental uniqueness/no-hit scans into Lean theorems.

`CanonicalMetric` proves the complex inner-product Gram defect is nonnegative and the stated algebraic curvature expression is nonpositive. Establishing that this expression is the curvature of the computed Bergman metric requires the missing analytic identification and regularity argument. Numerical periods, Abel–Jacobi coordinates, contour checks, quadrature and root continuation remain numerical.

Run `scripts/check_parallel_push.sh` to regenerate the exact fixtures, compile the needed existing dependencies and every new module sequentially, and check every new theorem for only the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` or a subset. The generator records source and generated-module hashes in `receipts/parallel_lean/inputs.json`; the axiom audit checks the exact expected declaration names, rejecting missing declarations, errors, custom axioms, `sorryAx` and `Lean.ofReduceBool`. Packet proofs are carried as actual proposition/proof pairs, allowing one axiom traversal per group. A separate temporary probe confirmed that a deliberately inserted custom axiom remains visible through this carrier. Exact-name coverage checks require every packet proof to be present, while the general theorems and matrix proofs are audited directly. Saved success metadata is in `receipts/parallel_lean/verification.json`. The repository Python suites are run separately with `make test`. Validation here is the focused compilation and axiom audit of the new modules; a full historical repository build was not rerun. The default library imports the new hand-written modules, and CI receives the dedicated reproduction step.

General effective Baker bounds, arbitrary-polynomial native completeness, number-ring solvers, arbitrary global function-field solvers, GPU/WASM deployment, enclosed analytic periods and smooth arbitrary-genus Voronoi boundaries remain open. Classical Chabauty still requires rank strictly below genus. The new algebra supplies proof infrastructure for these directions without claiming they have been completed.
