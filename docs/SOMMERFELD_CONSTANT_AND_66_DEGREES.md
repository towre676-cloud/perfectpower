# The Sommerfeld constant, the exact angle carrier and electromagnetic matching

## The constants and angles being compared

Sommerfeld's fine-structure constant is the electromagnetic coupling alpha, not the strong-CP determinant phase or the unitarity-triangle angle also conventionally called alpha. In SI units alpha = e-squared/(4 pi epsilon_0 hbar c). The latest available CODATA adjustment is 2022: alpha(0) = 0.0072973525643 with standard uncertainty 0.0000000000011, and alpha(0)-inverse = 137.035999177 with standard uncertainty 0.000000021. These are measured references, not inputs used to calibrate the new phase-orbit calculation. NIST's current constants table and the PDG 2026 electroweak review retain these values. The coupling at nonzero momentum is scale dependent; a proposed exact formula must specify the physical limit, renormalization convention and threshold matching.

The nominated scalar angle theta = 66 degrees is 11 pi/30 radians. It is distinct from the standard CKM phase delta, the unitarity-triangle angles, and the strong-CP phase theta-bar. The new Hermitian action protects theta-bar through first-order one-loop order while allowing weak CP. That protection does not identify its scalar source frame with an independently locked 66-degree field. An equation connecting those fields to quark matrices is still necessary. Comparing decimal constants or using the degree number 66 inside a coupling formula would not supply that equation; physically meaningful angle formulas must survive changing angle units.

The twelfth power maps the selected root to exp(2 pi i/5), so C = 1-z-to-the-twelfth-z-to-the-minus-twelfth = 1-2 cos(12 theta) = (3-sqrt(5))/2 = phi-to-the-minus-two. Its exact minimal polynomial is C-squared-3C+1 = 0. This is a genuine algebraic connection between the nominated angle and golden coefficient. It contains no electromagnetic coupling.

Moreover the positive-half-circle primitive-sixtieth-root angles yielding the smaller coefficient are exactly 6,66,114 and 174 degrees. All have negative partners with the same CP-even coefficient. The coefficient therefore loses the embedding information needed to distinguish 66 degrees. ## A photon theorem for the protected canonical action

Each complete up sector contains six Dirac states of electric charge +2/3, and each down sector six of charge -1/3. The ordinary doublet and vectorlike singlet components have the same electric charge within a sector. Consequently both chiral photon generators are Q_f times the six-dimensional identity. Independent left and right fermion diagonalization leaves each generator unchanged. The one-loop fermion photon vacuum polarization therefore depends only on the six singular masses; it cannot see their mixing eigenframes.

At spacelike momentum magnitude Q define the positive inverse-alpha screening contribution S_f(Q) = (2 N_c Q_f-squared/pi) sum_i integral_0^1 dx x(1-x) log[1+Q-squared x(1-x)/m_i-squared], with N_c = 3. Then alpha_eff(Q)-inverse = alpha(0)-inverse - sum_f S_f(Q) at the stated one-loop accuracy, omitting other particle contributions. This expression is a perturbative free-fermion contribution in declared model units. It does not replace physical hadronic vacuum polarization, lepton and charged-gauge contributions, or electroweak matching required to relate the model to measured alpha(0). The numerical integral independently passes the small-Q normalization S_f = N_c Q_f-squared Q-squared/(15 pi m-squared) for one mass, as well as scale covariance and zero-momentum checks.

An even stronger logarithmic identity follows from the triangular determinant. For D_f = [[v y_f I,0],[C_f,m_f I]], the product of all six positive singular masses is |det D_f| = (v y_f m_f)-cubed, independent of C_f. Hence sum_i log(mu/m_i) = 6 log(mu)-3 log(v y_f m_f). The complete one-loop logarithmic threshold, (2 N_c Q_f-squared/(3 pi)) times this sum in the declared integration convention, is source-independent even when C_f changes its eigenvalues. This exact statement requires retaining both light and heavy partners. Heavy-only thresholds can depend on the light masses; their dependence cancels against the light-partner contribution in the all-state logarithmic sum. The all-state logarithmic result does not imply that a finite-momentum polarization is independent of varying masses.

## The independent gauge boundary and the mechanism requirement

In the unit-charge covariant-derivative normalization the gauge action contains -k0 F_mu_nu F-to-the-mu-nu/4, where k0 = 1/e-squared and alpha-inverse = 4 pi k0. This operator is invariant under every declared finite flavor group, shaping charge and CP transformation. Its positive coefficient is independent of the scalar source-center coefficients. Equivalently, above electroweak breaking, alpha-inverse = 4 pi (g_Y-to-the-minus-two + g_2-to-the-minus-two) at tree matching. The finite flavor symmetry does not normalize either continuous gauge coupling.

A permitted scalar-dependent gauge kinetic interaction can create a dependence on scalar invariants, but it retains the independent constant k0. Specifying its coefficient to make 137.035999177 emerge would match the observed coupling, not predict it. Conversely inserting alpha into the source potential or fixing a source angle to 66 degrees would initialize the desired correlation. The new result precisely rules out using equal-charge one-loop mass thresholds as the missing frame-selection mechanism: complete photon screening remains unchanged along an allowed continuous weak-phase orbit, and its all-state logarithmic threshold is even independent of source eigenvalues.

A substantive extension must supply a justified UV gauge normalization or boundary condition, an interaction that fixes the source-center freedom, an embedding selector for the primitive-root angle, and their physical matching. A common scalar modulus alone supplies none of these coefficient relations automatically. Higher-loop weak/flavor corrections can carry mixing dependence and are not excluded by the one-loop theorem. They also require specified gauge and scalar inputs before they can predict a number. The current action therefore provides an exact golden-angle carrier and exact electromagnetic matching sum rules, not a derivation of the Sommerfeld constant from that angle.
