## The central opportunity

The reusable engine is a chain from an exact polynomial model to a finite or bounded decision: normalization, discriminants, rational quotient algebras, gcd and nonunit splitting, real root isolation, exact signs, constrained optimization and generated proof packets. In flavor physics its highest value is to replace a numerical fit or local minimum with a constrained, explainable statement: a coefficient relation forced by a mediator, an obstruction preserved by running, a global winner with an explicit remaining premise, or a rigorous bias/tension envelope. A better scan alone is weaker than a theorem or a new physical calculation. Polynomial tools do not manufacture missing thermal, gauge or cosmological input.

The latest flavor completion already has an exact scalar insertion map and a concrete protected singlet mechanism. The strongest remaining question is whether a correlated interaction derives and globally selects physically acceptable source frames, rather than accepting those frames as benchmark inputs. On a separate lane, the current spectator wall calculation supports exact thermal branches and conditional cosmological estimates, but it does not constitute the cosmology of that flavor mechanism. These two fronts can meet through a supplied dimensionful action and globally classified CP-related endpoints.
