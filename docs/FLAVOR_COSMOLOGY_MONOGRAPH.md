Finite-temperature transition and nucleation of the biased vacua. Two thermal questions remain open for the supplied model: whether the CP transition proceeds by bubble nucleation, and whether the late bias can remove the false vacuum by nucleation instead of network pressure. This section answers both. The declared thermal coefficient c=0.025000033333 equals the one-loop Debye value lambda/4+kappa/3 of the field content to every printed digit. Because phi is a gauge singlet, its one-loop potential is gauge independent; gauge fields enter only through Higgs loops suppressed by kappa=10^-7. The heavy mediator has M/T_c=4.94. That barrier is not controlled. Read literally, the resummed potential has degenerate minima at T_c=60768.72 GeV, 1.28 percent above T0=59999.96 GeV, with phi_c/T_c=0.0589. At the broken minimum, however, the resummed mass is m/T=0.0408 and the three-dimensional loop parameter lambda T/(8 pi m) equals 0.097431. Continuum linear stability of the coupled wall. The finite-box Cholesky test and the Jost census are numerical. Here the same conclusion becomes a theorem whose only inputs are qualitative properties of the profile. Let Phi=(u,y,eta) be a static solution with u odd and y, eta even. Suppose that on rho>0: (P1) 0<u<1, (P2) u'>0, (P3) y'<0 (needed only when g>0), (P4) eta'<0, and (P5) eta>0. Then the full Hessian operator L=-d squared+H(rho) on L squared(R) cubed is nonnegative and its kernel is spanned by Phi'. The angular-Higgs and gauge directions are also nonnegative. The reflection P commutes with L, so the two sectors can be treated separately.

Translation sector. Flip the signs of the S and eta components on rho>0: D=diag(1,-1,-1). The flipped operator D L D has off-diagonal entries -2 alpha u and -2 kappa u eta, both nonpositive. It is a cooperative system, and w=D Phi'=(u',-y',-eta') is positive with D L D w=0. Write each component as F_i w_i and integrate by parts. This gives the exact identity <psi,L psi>=sum_i integral w_i squared F_i' squared+(1/2)sum over i not equal j of |H_ij| w_i w_j (F_i-F_j) squared. The boundary terms vanish: at rho=0 the Neumann component has w_u'=u''(0)=0, and the Dirichlet components vanish linearly with positive slopes -y''(0) and -eta''(0). Hence L>=0 in this sector. Equality forces all F_i to be equal constants, because the couplings are nonzero for rho>0, so the kernel is exactly the translation mode. Numerically, the identity reproduces the directly evaluated quadratic form to 5 times ten to the minus eight. It gives zero, to 10^-10, for psi proportional to Phi'.

Opposite sector. Eliminate S and eta by a Schur complement, using two bounds. First, (-d squared+mu squared) inverse <= mu^-2. Second, the Higgs block satisfies H_eta eta=lambda_H(3 eta squared-eta0 squared+beta(u squared-1))>=m_h squared-kappa. The quadratic form is then bounded below by that of the scalar operator -d squared+U-nu. Here U=lambda(3u squared-1)+2 alpha r_S+kappa r_H+2 kappa beta u squared; the mediator term 4 alpha squared u squared/mu squared cancels exactly against the Schur remainder. Also nu=4 kappa squared u squared eta squared/(m_h squared-kappa). The u field equation gives exactly (-d squared+U)u=2(lambda+kappa beta)u cubed, so u>0 is a strict supersolution of -d squared+U-nu with margin 2(lambda+kappa beta)-4 kappa squared eta(0) squared/(m_h squared-kappa). That margin is 0.2 against a correction of 1.5 times ten to the minus thirteen. By the scalar ground-state identity the odd sector is positive, with no zero mode.

Hence -d squared+lambda_H r_H=(-d-eta'/eta)(d-eta'/eta) is nonnegative, even though r_H itself is negative inside the wall, down to -9.8 times ten to the minus eight.
