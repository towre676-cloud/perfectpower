# SPARK/GNATprove pilot: AdaCore's Von Neumann square root

This is the first GNATprove run in this repository. The workload is AdaCore's own regression
`QA31-008__von_neumann_sqrt`, pinned in
[`why3_isqrt/upstream/`](../why3_isqrt/upstream/AdaCore__spark2014/testsuite/gnatprove/tests/QA31-008__von_neumann_sqrt).
GNATprove (spark2014 `fsf-13`) was built from source here, with the GNAT 13.3 compiler, AdaCore's
Why3 fork, cvc5 1.1.2 and z3. All numbers are in [`receipts/summary.json`](receipts/summary.json).

| arm | level | proved | unproved | wall time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unmodified | 2 | 37 | 8 | 64 s |
| unmodified | 4, 60 s timeout | 37 | 8 | 10 min 20 s |
| selective ghost lemmas | 2 | 386 | 93 | 12 min 52 s |

**Baseline reproduced.** The unmodified program leaves exactly the checks that AdaCore's recorded
`test.out` lists:
- six loop-invariant preservations, at `p.adb:88`–`93`;
- the range check at `p.adb:98:26`;
- the postcondition at `p.ads:15:19`.

The one difference is that AdaCore's `test.out` also lists an implicit `Always_Terminates` line, which this build does not emit. Level 4 with a 60 s timeout proves nothing more.

**Selective support: not yet a gain.** [`selective/p.adb`](selective/p.adb) is generated by
[`gen_selective.py`](gen_selective.py) and keeps the original invariants and contract. It adds:
- a 16-row ghost table for `M`;
- a ghost partial root with `Y = R·M`, `UX = X − R²M` and `X < (R+1)²M`;
- two lemmas, one for a loop step and one for the shift invariants, each proved by a case split
  over the 16 rows with literal constants.

With it, the six original invariants are proved preserved, but only *relative to* the lemma
postconditions, which GNATprove does not discharge. The range check and the postcondition stay
unproved. **No AdaCore check is closed outright.**

The obstacles it found are concrete:
- `2 ** K` with a variable exponent is not evaluated even at a literal argument;
- products of the ghost integer need bounds the provers do not find.

PerfectPower's guarded-subtraction rule, which helps on the Why3 export, does not apply here: none of the eight checks is subtraction-shaped.

The selective attempt took about 36 minutes of edit-and-rerun. That excludes building GNATprove.

Reproduce: `spark_pilot/run.sh baseline 2`, `spark_pilot/run.sh selective 2` (needs `gnatprove`,
`cvc5`, `z3` on `PATH`).
