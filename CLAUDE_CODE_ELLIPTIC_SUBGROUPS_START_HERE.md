# Complete rational subgroup preimage handoff

The integrated operation computes generators for all H in E(Q) with pH in the subgroup generated by the supplied points, for p=2 or 3 and at most four source points. It enumerates complete projective coefficient lines and uses the existing complete prime fibres. Reduced row echelon pivot anchors replace the corresponding source points; the full rational p-kernel is added. Read docs/ELLIPTIC_SUBGROUPS_MONOGRAPH.md for the two-inclusion generation proof and its dependency-free treatment of source relations.

Run make elliptic-subgroups and make test. Production code is python/perfectpower/elliptic_subgroups.py, with replay in elliptic_subgroup_verifier.py, curve wrappers in elliptic_arithmetic.py and public dispatch in query_service.py. Ten preimage packets, a witness-independence packet and cold catalogue responses are in receipts/elliptic_subgroups. Every normalized coefficient line must be retained, each literal point sum must match its fibre target, and all work is charged against one request-wide root-node budget.

The hidden-index examples recover the P=(0,1),Q=(2,1) witness subgroup on y²=x³−4x+1 from P±Q or from 2P+Q,P+2Q. Follow-up packets show that this subgroup is closed under rational division by both 2 and 3. It is consequently saturated for every 2^a3^b. This is a specified subgroup, not a complete ambient group statement. Relation dimension alone is not an index when sources are dependent; the duplicate and four-zero examples enforce that distinction.

Useful next work is a generic Lean subgroup-generation theorem, finite-field relation semantics and point-law refinement; or an exact integer-presentation reducer that limits redundant and torsion generators during repeated extensions. Other primes need complete prime fibres. Repeated prime preimages do not by themselves prove termination or complete ambient rank.
