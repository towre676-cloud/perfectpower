# Noncoprime atlas intersection handoff

This push extends the concurrent explicit intersection implementation with separate factored simultaneous intersections for existing prime-power atlases and atlas products. Different polynomial sources are supported. Shared primes are resolved by maximal-power projection and every retained prime-group combination is composed by coprime CRT. The period is the LCM of input periods, and source scans require every original exact equation.

Run `make check-residue-atlas-intersection-factored` on Lean 4.20.0 with the pinned Mathlib. The generic foundation is `PerfectPower/ResidueAtlasIntersectionFactored.lean`; its fourteen audited declarations cover divisor projection, nested conjunction covers, coprime conjunction covers, exact root/cardinality identities, rectangle completeness/counts, empty obstructions and exact bounded source solutions. Literal worked certificates live in `receipts/residue_atlas_intersection_factored/*.lean`, and their input evidence, population results and complete small scans are in the corresponding JSON files. `validation.json` and `crosscheck.json` are rebuilt by the focused command.

The implementation is `python/perfectpower/residue_atlas_intersection_factored.py`. New JSONL operations are `intersect_residue_atlases_factored`, `verify_residue_atlas_intersection_factored`, `residue_intersection_factored_contains`, `residue_intersection_factored_population`, `residue_intersection_factored_select`, `residue_intersection_factored_rank`, `residue_intersection_factored_scan` and `native_residue_atlas_intersection_factored`. Inputs are one through eight complete atlas/product packets, at most 64 flattened constraints, maximal local modulus at most 256. Explicit representation limit is 1 through 100000; excess classes stay factored. Native generation inherits local modulus at most 32 and lifting-node budget 64, with a maximum 2048 prefix root combinations. It raises on unsupported certificate size instead of weakening the statement.

The monograph is `docs/RESIDUE_ATLAS_INTERSECTION_FACTORED_MONOGRAPH.md`. Its examples establish period 36 for overlapping periods 12 and 18, singular branch retention, exact simultaneous source filtering and a kernel-proved count in a square of radius ten to the fortieth. The original `residue_atlas_intersection.py`, its public operations, its three generic theorems and original corpus are preserved. This is an extension of the arithmetic atlas lane and preserves the concurrent Weil spectral work.
