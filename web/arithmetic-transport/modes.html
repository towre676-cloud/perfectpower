The new mathematical result

With two copies of each move, the tight-ceiling population has three routes and a nonzero signed coefficient when q ≥ p². When p < q < p², it has two routes whose contributions cancel exactly. That general threshold theorem is now proved in Lean.

The model supports several prime modes, rational heights and restrictions on move order. These are exact finite word-channel calculations. They do not assert measured quantum behavior of integers or a Riemann-hypothesis result.
