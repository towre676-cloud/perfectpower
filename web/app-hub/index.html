Different tools. A shared foundation.

NFL PerfectPower asks: what do the game history, scenario model and current prices say? Pokepolis asks: where will the next turn take me? Mathematical improvements can feed the tools that use them without treating a game, a sports estimate and an engineering calculation as the same kind of result.
